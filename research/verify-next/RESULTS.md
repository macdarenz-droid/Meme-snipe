## Changes to a spec

1. **OF-3, OF-4, OF-5: immutable releases (V9).** If immutable releases are on in the zeroed-data repository, a published `data-day-*` or `-k3` release can never be edited or deleted and its tag name can never be reused, even after deleting the release. The cards that say "the trimmed day goes out as a new release tag", "the `storage-stop` marker is cleared only by a reviewed change" and "the K2 release stays" must say which setting zeroed-data has. Either keep immutability off, or make every tag name single-use by design (for example `storage-stop-<n>` where the highest number is the live marker, and a re-publish after a failed upload uses a draft that is deleted before publish). Add a test that publishes through a draft.
2. **OF-4: the progress cache and the 10 GB limit (V10, V17).** An Actions cache entry not read for 7 days is removed, the repository's cache is 10 GB by default, and a runner has 14 GB of disk. "Progress cache deleted only after read-back" must also say what happens when the entry has already been evicted (resume from the stored units, not from zero), and a long owner wait after a storage stop must not rely on the cache.
3. **OF-2 markers and OF-4 scripts: cache write triggers (V11).** archive-check and the scan must run on `schedule`, `workflow_dispatch`, `push` or `repository_dispatch`. From `workflow_run` or any low-trust trigger the marker silently fails to save. Add a test that a failed marker save holds the chain (fail closed).
