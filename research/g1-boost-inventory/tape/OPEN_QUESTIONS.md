Points where `PREREG.md` and amendments 1 and 2 are silent or ambiguous. ## Rule and fills
- **OQ-1 Target in lamports.** §4 registers 85.005 SOL. The tape's non-mayhem completions sit at 85,005,359,057 lamports (`checks.json`, check 4, development units). **Used:** the registered 85,005,000,000; trigger 90% = 76,504,500,000. The 0.0004% difference cannot move a trigger by more than one trade.
- **OQ-2 First crossing.** "The first curve trade whose real SOL reaches 90%" is only known when an earlier state below the threshold is on the tape in the same run of contiguous units (an earlier trade, or the create). **Used:** otherwise the trigger is left-censored and not traded (reported in G1-0 (a)). A gap between units ends a run.
- **OQ-3 Neighbouring trades' fees.** **Used:** the last trade before the slot and every trade in it; the highest total rate. On the pool, BOOST slices (fee-free) are left out; so are buyback-authority rows and any row whose fee fields total 0 (red team R2-7). If no pool trade shows a rate, the snapshot tier at effective quote × `base_supply` ÷ base is used (amendment 3) and the trade is flagged (`pool_fee_source = fallback`).
- **OQ-4 Completion in the entry slot; infeasible size; skipped slot.** Our buy's order inside slot t0 + D is unknown, and the slot's end state is a completed curve. **Used:** a miss, counted not scored (`complete-in-entry-slot`). A size the curve cannot fill in either state is a miss for the primary and "infeasible" for §10 sizes. If slot t0 + D has no block, the states at that slot number are used and the entry time is the first produced slot at or after it.
- **OQ-5 Caps at the exit.** "The sell capped by the real vault": **Used:** the largest amount the vault absorbs is sold and the rest is valued at zero (`unsold_tokens`). Exit B on the curve has no registered cap: **Used:** a sell the curve's real SOL cannot pay, or a curve completed without a migration within 30 minutes, returns 0 SOL (`exit_ok = False`). Development saw one such case (a curve drained to 0.012 SOL).
- **OQ-6 Rent.** §4 asks for "the matching rent". **Used:** the account size from the create row's token program (Token-2022 170 bytes, SPL 165; unknown, i.e. created before the tape, 170) times the SIMD-0437 rate in force at the entry slot: 6,333 lamports/byte before epoch 1033 (slot 446,256,000, 2026-09-11 21:12 UTC), 5,080 from it (`docs/research/execution.md` F1). So validation days and 09-10 are charged 1,887,234 lamports, not the repo's 1,513,840; every trade logs `rent_mismatch_vs_repo`. **Ruled (amendment 3):** confirmed by date: 6,960 before 2026-09-03 (epoch 1028), 6,333 from it, 5,080 from epoch 1033; 170 bytes = 2,074,080 / 1,887,234 / 1,513,840 lamports.
- **OQ-21 Secondary exit delays.** "Exit delay 8 and 45 slots": **Used:** exit at m + 8 and m + 45; the entry delay stays 23.
- **OQ-13 Decomposition.** **Used:** curve leg = tokens at the last curve price − SOL paid; migration step = tokens at the pool's opening spot − tokens at the last curve price; window = exit proceeds − tokens at the opening spot (exit-A trades only).

## Gate G1-0 and BOOST rows
- **OQ-7 "Most graduates".** **Used:** more than half; a graduate with no BOOST counts as 0% spent after m + D; graduates whose BOOST window is not on the tape are left out (OQ-9); evaluated on all SOL universe graduates and on the triggered ones, and the gate kills if either fails.
- **OQ-8 "Spent after m + D".** A slice in slot m + D may land before our sell. **Used:** only slots strictly after m + D count.
- **OQ-9 BOOST window on the tape.** **Used:** complete when the vault reaches 0 or the run covers m + 4,500 slots; otherwise the share is not counted (descriptive horizon, not registered).
- **OQ-14 BOOST rows on S_amm; the cap argument.** The tape README says S_amm `protocol` flags `boost_buy_and_burn`, but it is 0 on every BOOST slice in the development units. **Used:** a BOOST slice is the S_amm row in the transaction (slot, tx_idx) and pool of a `BoostBuyAndBurnEvent`. The tape keeps no `boost_buy_and_burn` argument for successful slices; **Used:** the inner buy's `min_base_amount_out` on that row as `min_base_amount_burned` (UNVERIFIED mapping; F rows give the argument for failed slices only). In development every matched slice had a non-zero value. **Ruled (amendment 3):** amendment 2 governs; amendment 1's restate line is replaced (premise: a fixed buyer of about 17.6 SOL, up to its price cap); no new gate. Added descriptive row: per slice, cap headroom = (quote_amount_in ÷ min_base_amount_burned) ÷ (pool price just before the slice) − 1, by slice order and by slot after m (`gate.cap_headroom`, `boost_slices.csv`, `desc_cap_headroom`). The mapping stays UNVERIFIED. Decoder v3 sets `protocol = 1` on BOOST swaps; the event match is kept because it also covers older units.
- **OQ-22 Whole days.** G1-0's per-day counts assume every unit of the day is present. **Used:** the code reads whatever units it is given and lists them in `decisions_summary.json`; the gate must be run only on complete days.

## Amendment 1 (G1-HC)
- **OQ-11 Cluster details.** Links: W transfers (the tape keeps those ≥ 0.05 SOL) and T transfers of any mint, undirected, each pair dated by its first link. **Used:** an address with more than 50 distinct linked addresses as of the slot is neither joined through nor a member; a hub seed stays a member but joins nothing.
- **OQ-17 R details.** Base = all owners' tokens except the curve's (the creator included). Group 2: the owner's first buy of the mint at most 10 slots after the create. Group 3: known-cost tokens only; cost = SOL paid plus venue fees. Group 4: buys on every venue strictly before t0 + D − 1; "within 2 slots" = 0–2 slots after the bought mint's create or migration (a buy before a migration would need a later event). Gate (b): coverage per trigger = known-cost tokens held outside the curve ÷ tokens the curve has sold; **Used:** pass needs both the mean and the median at least 90%.
- **OQ-15 Interval for amendment 1 gate (a).** Not registered. **Used:** the same pool-clustered bootstrap stratified by day as amendment 2 (c) (10,000, fixed seed, percentile, one-sided 95%). Triggers without a migration have no "share sold" and are left out.
- **OQ-20 Flow definitions.** Pre-migration holders = holders at the end of slot m − 1; sold in [m, m + D] = their curve sells in slot m and pool sells, capped at each holder's holding. First-time buys = buys by owners with no earlier buy and no holding before m. SOL is the pool's vault change (`quote_amount_lp_adjusted`) or the curve's `sol_amount`.
- **OQ-23 Futility of the arms.** **Used:** an arm closes if its own one-sided 95% upper bound is below 0, or if G1 closes and its own bound is not above 0.

## Amendment 2 (G1-CAP)
- **OQ-12 N, λ and theme waves.** t = block time of the last produced slot at or before t0 + D − 1; grid t, t − 10 s, …, t − 3,590 s. Rivals: SOL-quoted curves not known to be mayhem (cashback coins are counted; the amendment does not exclude them), known from their first trade on the tape. λ needs the trailing hour inside one run of units, else the trigger is left out of G1-CAP. ## S0 and strata
- **OQ-19 S0 universe.** "The same universe" includes "curves that reach the trigger". **Used:** S0 draws one entry per G1 primary trigger mint (in universe, not censored), at a progress drawn from sha256(seed | day | mint). This conditions the control on later reaching 90%, which can only make S0 look better, so the lift test gets harder. S0 runs at the primary size and exit only.
- **OQ-10 Non-SOL strata trigger.** "Real SOL" does not exist for USDC- or token-quoted curves. **Used:** real quote ÷ completion quote, where completion quote = vq0·vt0/(vt0 − rt0) − vq0 (this gives 85.005 SOL for SOL curves); the same mayhem and cashback exclusions. Descriptive only.

## Checks
- **OQ-16 Check 3 (tier market cap).** The program was not read. Empirically, on the development units, the snapshot's tiers match the observed rates on 91.5% of canonical SOL trades with effective quote × 1B / base, 92.0% with effective quote × base supply / base, and 12.9% with the real vault alone (LP and protocol rates: about 98%, 98% and 13%). Creator rates vary per coin (overrides, uncharged creators). Pricing uses the observed rates, so the reading only matters for the fallback (OQ-3). **Ruled (amendment 3):** until the program is read, the fallback tier (OQ-3) uses effective quote × `base_supply` ÷ base on the snapshot tiers (`market.fallback_tier`).
- **OQ-26 Mayhem curves.** `virtual_sol_reserves − 30 SOL` is not real SOL on mayhem curves (negative values at completion). They are outside the universe; check 4 reads non-mayhem completions only.

## After the review (2026-10-08)
- **OQ-27 "A day" in amendment 1 gate (c) and amendment 2 gate (d). Ruled (amendment 4):** a count gate passes only if the pooled Step A count reaches the threshold and each Step A day reaches at least 40% of it (`gate.count_gate`). **Used:** the threshold is per day, so the pooled count must reach threshold × number of Step A days (the pooled daily average reaches it); a day with no units read counts as 0. G1-0 follows amendment 5: its kill is computed on the pooled Step A events, and any single day alone also kills (count rules at 40% per day, time and share rules at the full threshold on each day's own events). **Used:** the share rule groups graduates by the day they migrated, not the day of the trigger; the two differ only for a trigger that migrates the next day. On partial days the gate gives no verdict anywhere in gate.json (`g1.strip_verdict`).
