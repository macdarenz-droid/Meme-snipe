### Open questions (R1)
- **Q-R1-a (design owner): the payer-mass bar for the count rows and F1.** `PAYER_MASS.md` asks for "the median payer-attributed net buy SOL in the design's frozen hold window" against X* = Q × (√(1 + c) − 1), and 11 events a day at 2X*. For DEV-ZERO, REBUY-ANCHOR, SEAT-DRIFT and F1 it does not say: which flow is "payer-attributed" (the row's own net, first-time buyers only, or all buyers), which window is the "hold window" (the row's flow window?), which Q (each event's own effective quote, or U1's 419 SOL), and which cost c (the $5 round trip, 2.89%?). Until these are ruled, the code reports the bar as not computed and no row or gate passes.
- **Q-R1-b (design owner): DEV-ZERO arm order.** Is "then, in a fixed order" fixed-sequence testing (an arm counts only if the earlier ones pass, as now coded), or only an order of reporting with three separate verdicts?
- **Q-R1-c (lead): builder cards.** `H8_AMENDMENT_2.md` (universe floors, H11 chase check from 1-minute tape candles, H6, dust at migration, the extra sizes and the creator-fee-0 count) and `COUNT_ROWS_AMENDMENT_4.md` (slicer rows) need building before the H8 stratum or the slicer counts are read.
- **Q-R1-d (lead or partner): Design A open questions.** Rule on `edge-a/tape/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q1 and Q3–Q12 before `--score-primary` runs, above all Q5 and Q7.
