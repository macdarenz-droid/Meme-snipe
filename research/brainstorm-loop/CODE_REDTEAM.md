### Open questions (R1)
- **Q-R1-a (answered by COUNT_ROWS_AMENDMENT_7; follow-ups Q-R1-e to Q-R1-g)** (design owner): the payer-mass bar for the count rows and F1.** `PAYER_MASS.md` asks for "the median payer-attributed net buy SOL in the design's frozen hold window" against X* = Q × (√(1 + c) − 1), and 11 events a day at 2X*. For DEV-ZERO, REBUY-ANCHOR, SEAT-DRIFT and F1 it does not say: which flow is "payer-attributed" (the row's own net, first-time buyers only, or all buyers), which window is the "hold window" (the row's flow window?), which Q (each event's own effective quote, or U1's 419 SOL), and which cost c (the $5 round trip, 2.89%?). Until these are ruled, the code reports the bar as not computed and no row or gate passes.
- **Q-R1-b (answered by COUNT_ROWS_AMENDMENT_7: fixed sequence, as coded)** (design owner): DEV-ZERO arm order. Is "then, in a fixed order" fixed-sequence testing (an arm counts only if the earlier ones pass, as now coded), or only an order of reporting with three separate verdicts?
- **Q-R1-c (lead): builder cards.** `H8_AMENDMENT_2.md` (universe floors, H11 chase check from 1-minute tape candles, H6, dust at migration, the extra sizes and the creator-fee-0 count) and `COUNT_ROWS_AMENDMENT_4.md` (slicer rows) need building before the H8 stratum or the slicer counts are read.
- **Q-R1-d (answered by edge-a/AMENDMENT_2)** (lead or partner): Design A open questions. Rule on `edge-a/tape/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q1 and Q3–Q12 before `--score-primary` runs, above all Q5 and Q7.
- **Q-R1-e (design owner): DEV-ZERO payer flow.** Amendment 7 says "minus the median net of its matched controls", but DEV-ZERO's controls are not matched to events. Which controls belong to an event: all controls of the same arm, the same arm on the same day, or another match? And is the subtraction in SOL (event net SOL minus the controls' median net SOL, from pools of other sizes), or in shares of Q scaled by the event's Q?
- **Q-R1-f (design owner): REBUY-ANCHOR payer flow unit.** "The event's net rebuy flow (amendment 1)" is defined in amendment 1 as a share of effective quote, while X* is in SOL. Is the payer flow (event share − median matched mid-band share) × the event's Q, or the raw SOL flows subtracted? Which points are "the event": the top-quintile RB points?
- **Q-R1-g (design owner): F1 payer flow, Q and days.** Most leader buys are bonding-curve buys, which have no PumpSwap vault and virtual reserve, and F1's loader reads no reserves. Which Q and which tier apply to a curve event: the curve's real or virtual SOL, and pump's curve fee tiers? Which events count for "11 a day": day-2 buys of persistent leaders, as for the timing statistic? Is the placebo's late SOL counted from the placebo buy's own slot?
- **Q-R1-h (partner): return-test readings in R1-18, coded as the stricter choice; confirm or change.**
  - A trade whose entry or exit slot falls outside the covered tape is dropped and counted (`dropped`).
  - The stop watches swaps strictly after the entry slot.
  - A day read with no 420 trades fails "positive on each day".
  - The fixed cost uses the 170-byte account, since the mint's token program is not read.
  - A state without a chain reading cannot quote: an entry without one is dropped; an exit without one is a total loss.
