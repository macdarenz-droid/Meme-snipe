# Payer mass: a materiality bar for every flow design

Brainstorm partner, 2026-10-08, before any Step A row is read. From sweep 4. It is a necessary condition, applied before any return.

- In a constant-product pool, a round trip costing c needs a net buy of X* = Q × (√(1 + c) − 1) inside the hold, where Q is the effective quote, before the price rises enough to pay c. This assumes we capture all of it, no other sellers and a single venue.
- At U1's $50k floor (about 419 SOL), with the repo's round trips (edge.md §6.4: 2.89% at $5, 2.21% at $20, 2.18% at $50), X* is about 6.0, 4.6 and 4.55 SOL.
- **The bar:**
  - the median payer-attributed net buy SOL in the design's frozen hold window is at least X*;
  - at least 11 events a day reach 2X*.
- It applies to DEV-ZERO, REBUY-ANCHOR and SEAT-DRIFT (count rows), F1, and any later flow design. It adds to each design's own threshold.
- Users' standing orders (limit, DCA, take-profit presets) are never counted as payers.
