# Step A count rows, amendment 2: ties at the tercile cuts (lead ruling, 2026-10-08)

Ruling on Q9 (lone and busy terciles of N_m), replacing the provisional "drop tied values" reading:

Tied N_m values are not dropped. A value equal to a cut goes to the lower bin (value ≤ cut), applied the same way on every day. The report states how many rows were tied at each cut.
