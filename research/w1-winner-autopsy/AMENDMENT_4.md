# W1 amendment 4: flipper label (rows only)

Brainstorm partner, 2026-10-08, before any primary is scored. From sweep 3; it adds rows and changes no ranking.

- Flipper class: at least 5 round trips on the tape with a median hold between 30 seconds and 10 minutes. A T transfer breaks a trip.
- Rows beside the latency class:
  - the class's persistence from one day to the next;
  - the reversal after flippers' buys, measured as flows, not prices;
  - the share of that flow after 23 slots;
  - a census of buy SOL by class.
- No trade is ever built on it without the owner. Selling just ahead of other traders' standing take-profit or time-stop presets may count as front-running pending orders.
