# Portfolio-liquidation probe (H3): rules fixed before any return is computed

File: `research/liquidation-probe/PREREG.md`. This is exploration, not proof. It tests the outside reviewer's H3 (2026-10-08). An adversarial review on 2026-10-08 checked this text before commit; its fixes are folded in.

- **Stage 1** finds and classifies sales. It computes no return, and reads prices only up to each sale: the sale's own pre and post reserves, and bars that end at or before it.
- **Stage 2** scores outcomes, and runs only if the count gate passes. It is a separate script that reads only the frozen stage-1 table.
- A rule changed after this file is committed is a new trial.

## Question
A wallet at least 7 days old fully sells (90% or more) a third distinct token within 10 minutes, for SOL or stablecoins. That sale alone drops its pool's price by 3% or more. Does a $50 buy of that token after the real delay, sold 30 minutes later:
1. make money in SOL after all costs, and
2. recover more than a matched full exit of only that one coin?

## Prior, stated now
- **Coval & Stafford (JFE 86(2), 2007):** reversal needs widespread, verifiably forced selling and plays out over months. Isolated distressed selling showed −2.5%.
- **Ante (BRL WP 26, 2022):** on Uniswap, large sells are followed by further falls.
- **This repo:** sharp 5-minute drops bounce below the round trip.
- **Scout sample:** 2 of 20 full-exit sellers had H3's shape, and none moved the price by 3% or more.
- The prior is low. The most likely result is "unresolved: too rare".

## Universe (fixed now)
**U-PS:** the 41 canonical PumpSwap SOL pools of the deep-pool probe's groups A and B.
- Candidate bars come only from each pool's eligible days, with eligibility as of the previous day's close (deep-pool PREREG).
- The eligible days come from the full eligible list, regenerated by `research/deep-pool-probe/probe.py eligible` from GeckoTerminal daily bars, or from the research session's copy after a SHA-256 and count check (163 pools: A 25, B 16, C 122).
- The committed `eligible.json` lacks the per-day field.
- The committed `research/deep-pool-probe/universe.json` is the input. No pump.fun request is made.

**U-RAY:** the 42 constant-product pools in `research/cheap-venue-probe/universe.json` (36 Raydium AMM v4, 6 Raydium CPMM). Bars come from that probe's `fetch.py bars`.

**Excluded:** concentrated-liquidity pools.

**Bars:** 5-minute GeckoTerminal bars, with the SHA-256 of each file recorded.

**Fallback:** if U-RAY's bars cannot be downloaded within 72 hours of the start, stage 1 runs on U-PS alone. That decision is recorded before any classification.

## Period
Sales from 2026-07-22T00:00Z to 2026-09-21T13:29:00Z, so that both exits (t + 7 s + 30 min, and t + 60 s + 30 min) fall at or before the wall.

## Stage 1: find and classify sales
No return is computed.

**1. Candidate bars**
- 5-minute bars ending at or before the wall with (M − low)/M ≥ 3%, where M = max(the bar's high, the previous available bar's close).
- So a sale that drops the price 3% qualifies even when it is the bar's first trade, and later trades in the bar cannot decide whether it is found.
- Bars are processed in a seeded random order: SHA-256 of ("H3-v1", pool, bar start).

**2. Pool transactions**
- All successful transactions touching the pool in [bar start, bar end).
- Source: Helius `getTransactionsForAddress` (full detail, ascending, blockTime filter, `maxSupportedTransactionVersion` 1).

**3. Price before and after each transaction**
- PumpSwap: effective reserves from the decoded swap event (`decode_swap`): real quote plus virtual quote reserves.
- Raydium: the pool's two vault balances, found by vault address.

**4. Large sale**
- **Definition:** a transaction that sells the meme into this pool, where its own reserve-implied drop, 1 − P_after/P_before, is at least 3%.
- **Seller:** the token-account owner (not the signer) whose total balance of the mint fell the most. A token account closed in the transaction counts as 0 after.
- **Holdings before:** the owner's balance just before its first sale of that mint in the window.
- **Full exit, judged in this transaction:** the balance after it is at most 10% of the holdings before.
- **Proceeds, judged in this transaction:** the owner's SOL (native plus WSOL), USDC or USDT rose.

**5. Excluded sellers**
- the pool, its authority or its program;
- the mint's creator: PumpSwap `coin_creator`, or the fee payer of the mint's creation transaction;
- bots: more than 50 successful transactions in the window;
- wallets whose first transaction is less than 7 days before t.

**6. Seller window**
- Helius `getTransactionsForAddress` on the seller (full, `tokenAccounts: balanceChanged`, succeeded only).
- It covers transactions with block time ≥ t − 600 s that come strictly before the sale in (slot, index) order.
- **Another full exit:** one transaction in which the owner's balance of mint m falls to at most 10% of its holdings before, while the owner's SOL, USDC or USDT rises in that same transaction.
  - Any venue counts.
  - A mint sold over several transactions counts once its balance reaches 10% or less.
  - Excluded mints: SOL, WSOL, USDC, USDT, JitoSOL, mSOL, bSOL, jupSOL and INF.
- k = the number of distinct such mints, other than the current one.

**7. Groups**
Every group requires the current sale to be a full exit with SOL or stablecoin proceeds (step 4).
- **H3 event:** k ≥ 2.
- **Two-coin:** k = 1. Reported only.
- **Single-coin:** k = 0. This is the control pool.
- **Partial:** large sales that are not full exits or have no SOL or stablecoin proceeds. Kept in the table and reported only.

**8. Hold time**
- The first 20 checks use the `tokenTransfer` filter.
- If they return known acquisitions correctly, events and controls must also have held the mint for at least 1 h. Otherwise this rule is dropped.
- Either way, the decision is recorded before stage 2.

**9. Market state**
- m60 = the equal-weight mean log return of the universe pools over the 60 minutes ending at the last bar close at or before t.
- Terciles are taken over all stage-1 large sales.

**10. Stage-1 table**
- Every processed bar and every large sale goes into the table, with rejection reasons and the raw evidence.

## Count gate
The gate is fixed now and is decided before any outcome is read.
- **Early check after 2,000 processed bars:**
  - c̄ = credits spent ÷ 2,000;
  - B = 2,000 + floor((200,000 − credits spent) ÷ c̄);
  - P = H3 events found × min(all candidate bars, B) ÷ 2,000.
  - If P is under 100, stop. The verdict is **unresolved: too rare at an affordable cost**.
- **Continue** otherwise, in seeded order, until 150 events, the 200k stage-1 cap, or the end of the list.
- **Stage 2 runs only if all of these hold:**
  - at least 100 H3 events, each with at least 2 matched controls (events with fewer are dropped and counted);
  - spread over at least 30 distinct UTC days;
  - no coin holds more than 30% of the events.

  Otherwise the verdict is **unresolved**.

## Stage 2: trade
The same rules apply to events and controls.
- **Size:** q = 0.4193 SOL.
- **Entry:** the pool state after the last successful pool transaction with block time ≤ t + 7 s. Robustness line: t + 60 s.
- **Exit:** the pool state at or before entry + 30 minutes.
- **Fills:** constant product, with the fee taken on the input side.
  - PumpSwap: effective reserves. The fee is the lp, protocol and coin-creator basis points from the swap events at that time. A sale larger than the real quote vault counts as a total loss.
  - Raydium: the fee is read from the pool state or AmmConfig. CPMM includes the creator fee as an upper bound.
- **Fixed cost:** 414,009 lamports per round trip.
- **Net:** net = (SOL_out − q − fixed)/q.
- **Stress line:** net − 1 point.
- **Executable:** the round trip at entry is at most 1.5%. Trades that fail this are dropped and counted.

## Controls
**Single-coin full exits are the decisive control.** For each H3 event, take up to 5 single-coin sales with an exact match on:
- the drop bin: [3%, 5%), [5%, 10%) or ≥ 10%;
- the venue type (PumpSwap or Raydium);
- the depth tercile, using the real SOL reserve before the sale;
- the m60 tercile.

Among the matches:
- prefer the same pool first, then the nearest time within ±7 days;
- take at most one control per seller;
- break ties with seeds, as above.

**Market-adjusted line (secondary).** Each trade minus the equal-weight universe return over the same 30 minutes.

## Statistics and verdict
There is one primary test. For each event i:
- n_i = its net;
- c_i = the mean net of its matched controls;
- d_i = n_i − c_i.

Intervals: a 95% day-block bootstrap over UTC days of the sale, resampling each event with its own controls (10,000 resamples, seed 7), with a day-clustered t-interval beside it.

Verdicts:
- **Killed:** mean n ≤ 0, or mean d ≤ 0.
- **Promising:** all of the following hold.
  - The 95% lower bound of mean n is above 0.
  - The 95% lower bound of mean d is above 0.
  - The 60-second line's mean n is above 0.
  - The stress line's mean is above 0.
  - Leaving out any one coin still gives a mean d above 0.
- **Inconclusive:** anything else.
- **Unresolved:** the count gate failed.

Reported alongside:
- the two-coin and partial groups;
- the market-adjusted line;
- the share of events on days when the universe fell 10% or more;
- per-coin counts.

## Power
These figures are assumptions.
- The σ of a 30-minute return after a drop is assumed to be 3–6%, with DEFF ≈ 1.3.
- With 100–150 events, the SE of mean d is about 0.4–0.8 points.
- Only a lift of about 1–2 points can be detected.
- For comparison, the round trip is about 0.6% on Raydium and 0.9–1.0% on PumpSwap.

## Caveats (stated now)
- **Wallets are not owners.** One wallet emptied is not an owner emptied.
- **Informed sellers** would predict further falls.
- **Overlap with earlier probes.** The period overlaps the deep-pool and cheap-venue probes. The split by seller type has never been looked at.
- **Survivorship.** The pools were alive and deep on 2026-10-06/07, which favours rebounds.
- **Misclassification:** multi-hop routes, RFQ fills, Token-2022 fees and multiple token accounts. The table keeps the raw evidence for each sale.
- **Credits.** heli.py counts 10 per call. The docs meter `getTransactionsForAddress` at 10 per 100 full transactions, with a minimum of 10.

## Pre-data fixes (2026-10-08 Melbourne, 2026-10-07 UTC; recorded before any data pull)
The draft above is the registered design. These points make it executable; none of them uses data. Where a point narrows an ambiguity, the reading chosen is the literal one.

**Inputs**
- F1. **Eligible days.** No full eligible list with days exists in the repo or in this container. It is regenerated: GeckoTerminal daily bars for the 481 pools of `research/deep-pool-probe/universe.json`, then `research/deep-pool-probe/probe.py eligible` unchanged. U-PS = the regenerated pools with best group A or B. If the regenerated list differs from the committed one (163 pools: A 25, B 16, C 122), the regenerated list is used and the difference is reported. A pool's eligible days are all days in its `days` field (groups A, B or C as of the previous day's close). A candidate bar qualifies only if its start lies in an eligible UTC day.
- F2. **U-RAY.** The 42 pools of `research/cheap-venue-probe/universe.json` whose `venue` is `Raydium AMM v4` or `Raydium CPMM`. Every day of the period is eligible (that probe had no per-day rule).
- F3. **Bars.** Downloaded by `fetch.py` in this folder with the same GeckoTerminal requests as the two earlier probes (5-minute, `currency=token`, the meme priced in SOL, `before_timestamp` at the wall), at a 7-second pace with back-off on 429. Raw bars only: "the previous available bar" is the previous bar GeckoTerminal returned (a gap means no trades). SHA-256 per file is recorded.
- F4. **Period.** A candidate bar starts at or after 2026-07-22T00:00Z and ends at or before the wall (2026-09-21T14:00Z). A large sale with block time after 2026-09-21T13:29:00Z is kept in the table with the reason "after cutoff" and is never an event or a control.
- F5. **Seeded order.** Key = SHA-256 of the UTF-8 string `H3-v1|<pool>|<bar start unix seconds>`, sorted by hex digest ascending, over U-PS and U-RAY bars pooled.

**Helius**
- F6. **Method.** `getTransactionsForAddress` (gTFA) is smoke-tested with one call after this commit. If it works it is used as written (full mode, at most 100 transactions per page, ascending; signatures mode with limit 1 for existence checks). If the plan refuses it, the fallback is `getSignaturesForAddress` plus `getTransaction` with identical classification; the switch and its cost are recorded in RESULTS.md before any classification.
- F7. **Credits.** Own ledger (10 per call, as heli.py), hard stop at 250,000; stage 1 stops at 200,000 including the smoke test and the setup reads. At most 5 requests a second. Read-only methods only.
- F8. **Pool transactions.** gTFA on the pool address, `status: succeeded`, block time in [bar start, bar end).

**Classifying a sale**
- F9. **PumpSwap price.** For each transaction, the decoded Buy/Sell events of this pool in order. P_before from the first event's pre-swap effective reserves ((real quote + virtual quote) / base); P_after from the last event's post-swap reserves (post = pre ± base, pre ∓ `quote_amount_fee_adj`, as `research/execution-audit/audit.py`). The transaction sells the meme into the pool if its net base flow into the pool is positive.
- F10. **Raydium price.** The pool's two vaults are found once per pool: the pool account is read (`getAccountInfo`) and the vaults are the two token accounts, owned by the Raydium authority, whose addresses appear in the pool account's data and in a pool transaction's token balances (one with the meme mint, one with WSOL). Price = WSOL vault / meme vault from `preTokenBalances` and `postTokenBalances`. Accrued protocol, fund or creator fees in the vaults are not subtracted (a stage-1 approximation, reported as a caveat). The transaction sells the meme into the pool if the meme vault rises.
- F11. **Seller.** Per owner, the sum over that owner's token accounts of the mint's balance, before and after (an account missing after counts 0). Owners excluded as the seller candidate: the pool, the pool's vault authority, the AMM program. The seller is the remaining owner with the largest fall; no fall means "no seller found".
- F12. **Proceeds.** In the same transaction, the owner's native SOL change (if the owner is an account key) plus its WSOL token change is above 0, or its USDC (`EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v`) or USDT (`Es9vMFrzaCERmJfrF4H2FYD4KCoNkY11McCe8BenwNYB`) change is above 0. A rent refund alone can satisfy the SOL test; the raw deltas are kept.
- F13. **Holdings before (current sale).** The seller-window transactions of step 6 plus the sale itself, in (slot, index) order: the owner's balance of the mint just before the first transaction in which that balance fell with proceeds (F12). Full exit if the balance after the current sale is at most 10% of that.
- F14. **Other full exits (k).** The same test for every other mint in the seller window: a "sale" is a transaction where the owner's balance of mint m falls and F12 holds; holdings before = the balance before m's first sale in the window; m counts once some sale leaves the balance at most 10% of that. Excluded mints: SOL/WSOL, USDC, USDT, JitoSOL `J1toso1uCk3RLmjorhTtrVwY9HJ7X8V9yYac6Y7kGCPn`, mSOL `mSoLzYCxHdYgdzU16g5QSh3i5K3z3KZK7ytfqcJm7So`, bSOL `bSo13r4TkiE4KumL71LsHTPpL2euBYLFx6h9HP3piy1`, jupSOL `jupSoLaHXQiZZTSfEWMTRRgpnyFm8f6sZdosWBjx93v`, INF `5oVNBeEEQvYi1cX3ir8Dx5n1P7pdxydbGF2X4TxVusJm` (addresses as known to the builder; an error here only affects LST sales).
- F15. **Bots.** Counted over the seller window [t − 600 s, the sale): more than 50 successful transactions. Paging stops once 51 are seen.
- F16. **Wallet age.** "First transaction less than 7 days before t" is tested as: no transaction of the owner (any status) with block time ≤ t − 7 days (gTFA signatures, limit 1). The two are equivalent.
- F17. **Creator.** Both tests apply to every pool: the PumpSwap `coin_creator` from the decoded swap event (PumpSwap pools), and the fee payer of the mint's oldest transaction (gTFA on the mint, full mode, ascending, limit 1). Checked once per mint, only when the mint has a full-exit sale.
- F18. **Order of checks.** The seller window (step 6) is fetched for every large sale with a seller and proceeds, because "holdings before" (F13) needs it; at most 3 pages of 100, and a window with more than 50 transactions before the sale is a bot. Wallet-age, creator and hold-time checks run only for full exits with proceeds. Partial large sales keep the step-4 evidence. *(Amended 2026-10-08 before stage 1 ran; only the gTFA smoke test, 5 calls on one pool, had been made.)*

**Hold time and market state**
- F19. **Hold-time rule (step 8).** Held at least 1 h means: the owner has an incoming transfer of the mint with block time ≤ t − 3,600 s (gTFA signatures, `tokenTransfer {mint, direction: in}`). Validation on the first 20 full-exit sales in seeded order: a second query with block time ≤ t, descending, limit 1, in full mode (the same transaction `getTransaction` would return). "Correct" = all 20 return a transaction that raises the owner's balance of the mint. All 20 correct → the rule is adopted for events and controls; otherwise it is dropped. The decision is written to the stage-1 table before the early check.
- F20. **m60.** Equal-weight mean, over all universe pools whose raw bars span both times (closes carried across gaps), of log(close at the last bar end ≤ t / close 60 minutes earlier). Terciles over all stage-1 large sales with an m60 value.

**Gate**
- F21. **Early check.** "H3 events found" counts H3 events after every exclusion (and the hold-time rule if adopted) and before control matching. "Credits spent" is this probe's ledger total. If fewer than 2,000 candidate bars exist, the early check is replaced by processing all of them.
- F22. **No outcome before the gate.** Stage 1 reads no bar or reserve after a sale's own transaction. The one exception is step 1's bar screen on the full bar's low; it cannot decide whether a sale is found, because any sale with a 3% own drop leaves the low at or below 0.97 × M.
