## Round 3 (head `3998fe5e`): red team FAIL (1 MAJOR, 1 MINOR); reviewer pending

- Red team MAJOR (`queue.ts` admit): ruling 12's "no poll in the current minute" is a calendar-minute test. In the first seconds of each minute every pool looks idle, so one new candidate evicts a live pool. The evicted pool's next poll evicts another, a cascade of 30 evictions every minute. The last live pool is refused with `E_POOL_CAP` and its snapshot is lost. Side effects: duplicate (pool, minute) `poll_counts`, false `changedPolls` after re-admission, and a position pool can be the victim. The supervisor's wording in ruling 12 caused this.
