## Round 3 (head `3998fe5e`): red team FAIL (1 MAJOR, 1 MINOR); reviewer pending

- Red team MAJOR (`queue.ts` admit): ruling 12's "no poll in the current minute" is a calendar-minute test. In the first seconds of each minute every pool looks idle, so one new candidate evicts a live pool. The evicted pool's next poll evicts another, a cascade of 30 evictions every minute. The last live pool is refused with `E_POOL_CAP` and its snapshot is lost. Side effects: duplicate (pool, minute) `poll_counts`, false `changedPolls` after re-admission, and a position pool can be the victim. The supervisor's wording in ruling 12 caused this.
- Red team MINOR (`queue.ts` notePollFailed): a failed poll of a position pool is refused at the cap, so the open position gets no `poll_counts`.

### Supervisor rulings for round 4 (8 Oct 2026, 6:28 PM; ruling 17 replaces ruling 12's idle test)

17. **MAJOR.** A pool is idle only by elapsed time: `nowMs - lastPollMs ≥ max(60 s, 3 × its poll interval)`. Use the pool's own interval where the queue knows it; otherwise use the slowest configured interval. Never by calendar minute. A pool last seen on `pool_snapshot_position`, or with an open position, is never a victim.
18. **Invariant.** No (pool, minute) record is ever written twice; flush a victim only up to the current `minute`. A poll with no previous hash in its watch counts as unchanged, because "changed" needs two hashes. Record that in DECISIONS.
19. **MINOR.** `notePollFailed` admits a position pool always (stream or position flag). Test: cap full of pools polled this minute, then a failed poll of a position pool is counted.
20. **Test** the red team's repro: 30 pools at 1 Hz and 30 at 0.1 Hz, cap 60, a new candidate at minute start + 1 s. Expect 0 evictions of live pools, 0 refusals, 0 duplicate pool-minutes, 0 false `changedPolls`. Also: a truly idle pool (no poll for 3 intervals) is evicted, and a position pool never is.
