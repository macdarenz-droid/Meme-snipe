Paper only

The bot holds no signing key, and the worker has no way to send a transaction; any mode other than paper stops it at start. Each paper entry and exit is built as a real transaction and simulated on mainnet, never sent. Today nothing can enter, even on paper and in a practice run: the market-conditions check stays off, and checks H14 and H15 reject until their inputs are wired. After that, until research proves an edge, the cost check refuses every entry, so only a practice run trades on paper.

Exits never wait

Halts, your /pause and loss limits stop new entries only. Exits are always allowed, are served first from the call budget, and still run at 70% of the monthly credits. A sell is retried up to 5 times with fees rising from 0.00002 to 0.0005 SOL; after that the bot marks 'exit blocked' and keeps watching.

Loss limits

Each trade's stop sits at most 20% below the price after the buy. These stop new entries; open trades can still exit: daily loss of 7.5% of the bankroll (until midnight Melbourne), 2 losses in a row (2 h), 3 in a row (rest of the day), weekly loss of 20% (until the week ends and you review), 5 losses in 20 trades (until you review), kill switch at 70% of the peak (until you re-arm). If the heartbeat is over 90 s old, the watchdog alerts you, but it cannot sell: while the bot is down, an open trade has no working stop. Restart drills measure that gap.

What only you do

Fund the bot wallet and move money, switch paper to live, set the live risk limits, re-arm the kill switch, review a weekly-loss or 5-in-20 pause, clear a /pause at the server console (zeroed-resume), add or rotate API and wallet keys, approve paid services (such as the Helius full pull, at most US$99), approve personal data or data sent to a third party, and loosen any proof threshold. No code or agent does these. Keys live in GitHub secrets and in locked files on the server, never in the repo or in chat.
