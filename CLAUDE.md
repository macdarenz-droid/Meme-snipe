## Product
- Zeroed trades only when the data proves the setup (owner, 2026-10-03): consistent, risk aware, data aware, built on research into what matters in the market. Missing, stale or unproven evidence means no trade.
- Capital and trade size scale (owner, 2026-10-03). The $20 bankroll and $2–$5 trades are only the trial setting. Bankroll, minimum and maximum trade size, loss limits and position count are configuration, never constants in code or UI. Quotes and costs must stay exact at any size (price impact from pool reserves at the real size, depth caps that scale with the pool). Limits rise only when the owner raises them after the bot has proven itself; code never raises them on its own.
- No deposit before proof (owner, 2026-10-03; changed the same day: backtest on historical data instead of waiting 7 days). Before the owner funds the app, the engine and its logic are tested many times on real data. Pre-funding gate, all required, evidence kept in the repo:
  1. Deterministic replay: the same market data replayed 10 times gives identical decision logs.
  2. Historical backtest: at least 30 days (target 60 or more) of survivorship-free historical on-chain data, replayed transaction by transaction through the same engine code the live bot runs, with zero crashes, illegal states or unreconciled intents.
  3. Live dry run: the full worker on live feeds for at least 48 hours, started as soon as the worker exists and run in parallel with other work (no separate waiting), with restart and disconnect drills during it; at least 99% uptime; every decision logged with its reasons.
  4. Dry-run execution: during the live dry run, every paper entry and exit is built as a real transaction and simulated against mainnet (never sent); at least 95% simulate successfully and the simulated amounts match the local quote within tolerance.
  5. Fault injection: timeouts, stale feeds, rate limits and restarts mid-trade all pass the acceptance cases.
  6. A proven strategy: in the historical backtest, rules fixed in advance, walk-forward, and a later untouched holdout with at least 300 out-of-sample trades whose 95% confidence interval of mean net return is above zero (sample size designed for 80% power); and the live dry-run paper trades stay consistent with the backtest's expected results. Without one, the app stays in paper mode and asks for no deposit.
- Backtests are blind and reproduce live (owner, 2026-10-03). The engine only ever sees data up to the current simulated moment: events arrive in slot and time order through a simulated clock, every lookup is "as of" that moment, and future data and outcomes are physically out of its reach (outcomes are scored in a separate stage the engine cannot read). Backtest and live run the same engine code; only the data feed and clock are swapped. Proof required: a leak test that plants a future-only marker and fails if any module can see it early, and a parity test where data recorded during the live dry run is replayed through the backtester and must reproduce the live decisions exactly.
- No AI wording in the UI (owner, 2026-10-03). Every word in the app reads as written by a person. Never mention AI, models, assistants or "smart"/"intelligent" features, and never use stock AI phrasing such as "at a glance", "seamless", "effortless", "unlock", "elevate", "empower", "leverage", "delve", "dive in", "robust", "cutting-edge", "harness", "supercharge", "streamline", "insights", "journey", "game-changer", "powered by", "Let's", "Here's", or decorative sparkles. Use short, specific labels a trader would write ("Today", "Open trade", "Daily loss"). A guard test in the web app fails the build on any flagged word; add to its list, never remove from it.
- Two themes only (owner, 2026-10-03): Paper and Silent Black (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- Name and logo (owner, 2026-10-03): Zeroed, "Slot" mark (see `docs/BRAND.md`).
- Funding (owner, 2026-10-03): Deposit and Withdraw offer two exchanges to choose from, Independent Reserve and Kraken; the bot only sends to the owner's saved wallet.
- Free tiers only for hosting extras (owner, 2026-10-03): no custom domain and nothing paid on Cloudflare. The watchdog runs on the free Workers plan at its `workers.dev` address. Live dashboard access from the owner's phone uses a free private network (Tailscale Personal) instead of a public domain, so the server keeps no open inbound ports.
